Privacy policy and terms of use

“Zas” app · last updated 3 October 2026 · terms version 2 · Versión en español (the Spanish version prevails in case of doubt)

In one line: this app collects no data. There is no server, no user account, and nothing you save leaves your phone. The only possible connection is an Open Food Facts lookup that you must switch on yourself, and only the barcode is sent. No cookies, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, on Android and on iPhone.

The first time you open the app you accept these terms with one tap. The date and version you accepted are stored on your phone; if they change, you are asked again.

  2. It is not a medical service or a medical device. The people behind Zas do not act as doctors, pharmacists, dietitians or religious authorities. Zas does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease and does not replace their advice.
  3. The package, your doctor and your pharmacist come first. Source data may contain errors or be out of date. If in doubt, check the product label and ask a professional. Never stop or change a treatment because of what Zas says.

  5. No guarantee of completeness. Zas warns about what it finds; the absence of a warning does not guarantee that a product does not contain something.
  6. Age. Zas is intended for people aged 14 and over, the age from which a person can consent to data processing in Spain (Article 7 of Organic Law 3/2018). Data about a child or a baby is entered by their parent or guardian.

  9. Manufacturers and errors. Zas does not judge brands: it states what the label declares and which public criterion applies, with its source. If a product's data is wrong, it is corrected at its source (Open Food Facts or AEMPS) and Zas picks it up in its next version. Manufacturers and users can tell us at the contact address; we reply and, if there is an error, we correct it.

Special categories of data

Allergies, intolerances, medical conditions and the “Quit smoking” log are health data, and some food preferences may reveal beliefs. Article 9 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) treats them as special categories. The app therefore asks for your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)), separate from any other acceptance, before storing anything; “Quit smoking” has its own separate consent and its own delete button. You can withdraw consent at any time from Profile › What you avoid › Delete my exclusions; your exclusions are deleted immediately.

The optional Open Food Facts lookup

When a product is not in the database bundled with the app, you can ask it to look it up on Open Food Facts. It is off by default; you switch it on with the Search online toggle on the scan screen. Only the barcode is sent, over HTTPS: never your allergies, history, prices or any identifier. A barcode identifies a product, not a person. Open Food Facts is a non-profit based in France, inside the EU, so there is no international transfer.

Who else sees your data

Nobody. With the lookup off, nothing is sent. Barcode and text recognition run on the device (ML Kit on Android, with its usage statistics to Google switched off; Apple's Vision framework on iPhone). Android cloud backup is disabled (android:allowBackup="false") and on iPhone your data is excluded from iCloud backup.

No cookies, no ads, no tracking

The app uses no cookies, advertising identifiers, analytics or third-party tracking kits, and shows no ads. On iPhone it will not ask for tracking permission because it tracks no one. This website uses no cookies or analytics and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which logs visitors' IP addresses for security, as stated in its privacy statement.

App stores

When you download Zas, Google Play or the App Store process your account and download data as independent controllers under their own policies. Zas receives no data about you from them.

Medicines and supplements

If you scan a medicine box, the app looks it up in the list of medicines marketed in Spain published by the Spanish Agency of Medicines and Medical Devices (CIMA), which is bundled inside the app: the lookup never leaves the phone. It says whether it contains alcohol or anything of animal origin, whether it contains something you avoid and, if you enabled it, what your religion says, with its citation. It does the same for supplements from their ingredient list. This is information, not medical advice. Do not stop or change a treatment without talking to your doctor or pharmacist. Source: AEMPS, www.aemps.gob.es; for medicines authorised across the EU, the European Medicines Agency, www.ema.europa.eu.

Baby on board, recipes and nutrition

Baby criteria come from published bodies, always cited: the WHO Europe nutrient profile model for foods for infants aged 6–36 months (2022), the WHO recommendation of exclusive breastfeeding up to 6 months and the AESAN Scientific Committee report on infant botulism. Zas recommends no product on its own and does not replace your paediatrician. Recipes are original or from Wikibooks (CC BY-SA 3.0); nutrition values are calculated from USDA FoodData Central (public domain) and claims use the thresholds of Regulation (EC) 1924/2006. No decision about allergens, diets or religious restrictions comes from an artificial-intelligence model.

Permissions

Camera: only for scanning, requested when first needed; if you refuse, the app still works and you can search products and medicines by name in Search. Photos: no gallery permission; the system photo picker hands over only the photo you tap. Internet: only for the optional Open Food Facts lookup. No location, contacts, microphone or notifications.

Retention and your rights

Your data stays until you delete it or uninstall the app; More › Legal › Your data has a button that erases everything at once. Because the data is only on your device and the developer has no access to it, you exercise access, rectification and erasure yourself in the app. You can still write to the contact address, and you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your local supervisory authority.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and asks for no identifying data. Data about a child or baby is entered by the parent or guardian, stays on the phone and can be deleted at any time.
